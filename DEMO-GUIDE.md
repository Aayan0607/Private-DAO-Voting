# Simple demonstration guide — 2 to 3 minutes

Record the REAL local MVP at localhost:3000. Keep the local chain running. Use disposable identities and save the first identity's encrypted backup for the retry scene. Trim waits and window changes. The hosted preview can be mentioned briefly at the end, with its simulation label visible.

## Introduction (15 seconds)

“I’m Aayan Tamboli from Team Stupefy. This is Private DAO Voting. Approved members can vote without publicly linking their membership to their choice. I’m demonstrating the real local blockchain version.”

## Membership (25 seconds)

Create an identity, save its backup and approve its public ID in the demo group.

“My private identity stays in my browser. I can save an encrypted backup. The administrator approves only my public membership ID. Creating an identity alone does not grant voting access.”

## Proposal (20 seconds)

Publish a workshop-funding question and open voting.

“This proposal fixes who can vote and when voting ends. Adding someone later cannot change this existing proposal.”

## Vote and receipt (35 seconds)

Choose Yes, generate the proof, submit and show confirmation.

“The proof confirms that I’m an approved member without identifying which member I am. The relay sends it to the contract. Here is the real local transaction receipt and updated tally. Refreshing keeps the result.”

## Duplicate rejection (25 seconds)

Restore the same identity in a clean incognito session and try the opposite choice on the same proposal.

“Now I try again from a fresh browser session. The second vote is rejected. Changing browsers or changing the answer does not create another vote.”

## Unapproved and late member (30 seconds)

Create an unapproved identity and show its denial. Approve it, show continued denial on the original proposal, then eligibility on a new proposal.

“This new identity is not approved, so it cannot vote. Even if I approve it now, the existing proposal stays unchanged. A new proposal can include the updated member list.”

## Closing (15 seconds)

“The local MVP uses real proofs and smart contracts. Our hosted interactive preview lets visitors explore the same workflow with clearly labelled simulated results. Ballot choices are public; the proof conceals the membership-to-ballot link. No MST testnet deployment is claimed. Thank you.”

## Final checks

Keep the final video under three minutes; inspect audio and screen readability. Show the final receipt only after confirmation. Avoid recording private identity storage or terminal keys.
